About Us

Research is a discipline. We treat it like one.

At The Research School (TRS), we bridge the gap between academic research, industry insight, and practical application. We are a research-driven platform that supports academicians, scholars, and organizations by providing end-to-end research solutions, training, and mentorship in today's data-driven and AI-enabled world.

What We Do

Four ways we support your research journey

From raw data to a finished publication, from a single workshop to an AI-enabled research practice — here's how we help.

Market Research & Data Collection

Reliable, high-quality data collection for:

  • — Academicians working on research papers, theses, and funded projects
  • — Organizations seeking actionable consumer and market insights

Our expertise ensures accurate, ethical, and research-grade data that strengthens decision-making and publication outcomes.

Mentorship for PhD & DBA Scholars

Structured guidance to help scholars:

  • — Refine research ideas and frameworks
  • — Design robust methodologies
  • — Analyze data and interpret results
  • — Publish in high-quality journals

Our goal is simple: help you finish strong and publish with confidence.

Workshops on Research Tools

Hands-on training programmes on:

  • — Quantitative tools (PLS-SEM, SEM, SPSS, etc.)
  • — Qualitative research methods
  • — Research design and academic writing

Each workshop is designed to be practical, interactive, and immediately applicable.

AI in Education & Research

Helping academicians and students leverage AI to:

  • — Accelerate literature reviews
  • — Improve research writing and structuring
  • — Enhance data analysis and interpretation
  • — Increase research productivity

Our focus is on ethical and effective use of AI, ensuring technology enhances — not replaces — critical thinking.

Our Approach

Methodology first, always

We don't start with a template — we start with your question. Every engagement, academic or commercial, is scoped around what you actually need to know and how defensible the answer needs to be. That means the right method, the right sample, the right analysis, and a result you can stand behind — in a viva, a board meeting, or a published paper.

We work as mentors as often as we work as executors. For scholars, that means walking alongside you rather than handing back a finished product you don't fully own. For organizations, it means being transparent about method and limitations, not just delivering a polished deck.
