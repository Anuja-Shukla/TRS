Article 1 · Press & Social Coverage

MSME Credit-Readiness Capacity-Building Programme

Assam 25–30 August 2026

Intellique Research School and Asia Pacific Consulting & Advisory (APCA), in collaboration with the Industries and Commerce Department, Government of Assam, and with support from The Asia Foundation, ran a series of MSME Credit-Readiness Capacity-Building Workshops across Guwahati and Tezpur, Assam. The programme brought together almost 200 women-led MSMEs for practical guidance on accessing formal finance, credit documentation, CIBIL and responsible borrowing — and was picked up by regional press including The Assam Rising, Amar Guwahati, and other Assamese-language dailies, alongside coverage on social media.

Article 2 · Press Coverage

Easing Access to Formal Credit for Small Businesses — Panel Discussion, Guwahati

Guwahati, Assam 21 September 2026

A regional Assamese-language daily covered a special panel discussion held in Guwahati on making formal institutional credit more accessible to small and medium businesses, organised by Asia Pacific Consulting & Advisory (APCA) and Intellique Research School (IRSPL) as part of the same MSME credit-readiness initiative. The panel brought together representatives from banks and financial institutions, government, academia, and the small-business community to discuss practical steps for improving credit linkage for small entrepreneurs.
